Where the 24/7 line sends plumbers

.

We dispatch from Los Gatos to five nearby communities. Campbell, Saratoga and Monte Sereno are neighboring towns, Lexington Hills is an unincorporated community, and Cambrian Park is a neighborhood on the south side of San Jose. Each has its own page with local detail and the ZIP codes we treat as that town.

Pick your town from the cards below. If your address sits on a border, such as a street that crosses from Los Gatos into Campbell or Cambrian Park, give the dispatcher the exact address. Mailing city and actual jurisdiction do not always match, and it changes which sewer district and building office applies.

Around Los Gatos itself, ZIP codes 95030 and 95032 are the main ones, and 95030 is shared with Monte Sereno. ZIP 95033 belongs to Lexington Hills, and the same line also answers calls from 95031.

Why the town you live in matters for plumbing

.

Plumbing is local in ways people do not expect. West Valley Sanitation District serves Los Gatos, Campbell and Monte Sereno, plus about two thirds of Saratoga, so a sewer backup in those towns starts with a call to the district. Where your house sits in that service area decides who clears which part of the lateral.

Water also varies. San Jose Water lists Los Gatos, Campbell, Saratoga and Monte Sereno in its service area map, and its report gives different hardness numbers for mountain, groundwater and Valley Water supply. For that reason, we tell you to test your own tap before choosing a softener or a tankless heater.

What changes between one town and the next

.

Building permits are issued by the town or city where the house stands, so a water heater replacement in Los Gatos goes through the Town of Los Gatos portal, while another town has its own office and its own steps. Permit rules vary, so ask the local building division before work starts.

Housing and utilities differ by town too. Campbell has a large group of 1960s and 1970s houses, Lexington Hills has wells and septic systems, and Saratoga splits its sewer service between two districts. Each town page covers those details, so read yours before you call.
