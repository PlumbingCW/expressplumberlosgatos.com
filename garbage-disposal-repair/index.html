Disposal leaking or jammed: first steps

Water under the sink, a burning smell or a disposal that will not stop humming is worth an urgent call, and our line answers 24/7. A few safe steps will limit the damage while you wait.

  1. Switch the disposal off at the wall switch and unplug it under the sink, or turn off its breaker..
  2. Never put a hand into the disposal, even when it is off. Use tongs or pliers to lift out anything visible..
  3. If water is leaking, close the two angle stops under the sink, clockwise, and put a bucket under the unit..
  4. Stop using the sink, and call (855) 575-2890 if the leak or the jam continues..

Garbage disposal repair in Los Gatos starts with a simple question, which is whether the problem is the motor, a jam, a leak or the drain behind it. A disposal grinds food into small pieces, but it relies on the sink trap and the two-inch drain line to carry them away. A disposal that sounds dead can turn out to be a clog downstream.

It hums but the blades do not turn

.

A steady hum with no spinning means the flywheel is jammed, usually by a bone, fruit pit or piece of silverware. Many units have a hex socket in the center of the bottom. A quarter-inch Allen key turned back and forth frees the flywheel.

After freeing it, press the red reset button on the bottom of the unit. If the motor trips again right away, the winding may be burned out and replacement is cheaper than repair.

A kitchen sink that backs up with the disposal running

.

A disposal pushes ground food into the drain, and grease in that food cools into a paste on the pipe wall. In many older kitchens the branch line is two inches wide and runs under the floor to the main. Coffee grounds, rice and pasta swell and settle in the trap.

If water rises in the other sink basin while the disposal runs, the clog is past the unit. A cable run through the trap or the cleanout clears it, and drain cleaning with a camera finds the cause when it keeps coming back. Repeat clogs can point to a belly in the line, which jetting cannot cure.

The dishwasher connection

.

Most disposals have a knockout plug on the side for the dishwasher drain hose. If it was never punched out during installation, the dishwasher will not drain. A kinked hose or a clogged air gap on the counter causes the opposite problem, with water spilling out of the air gap cap.

Both checks take a few minutes during a disposal visit, and a backed-up dishwasher is a common reason a sink smells.

West Valley Sanitation District, which serves Los Gatos, says to flush only toilet paper and human waste. The same sense applies at the sink. Installing a new disposal

.

Replacement means disconnecting the dishwasher hose and drain elbow, releasing the mounting ring, and fitting the new unit to the sink flange. The electrical side is a plug or a hard-wire connection to a switched outlet. If the sink has no outlet, an electrician or a code-approved connection is needed, and permit rules vary, so check with the Town building division.

While the cabinet is open, we replace worn angle stops and braided lines, since those fail on the same schedule. A similar fix is covered under faucet repair.
