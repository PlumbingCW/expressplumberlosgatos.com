What to say when you call

.

Give your town and ZIP code first so the right area is matched. Then describe the problem in plain words, such as water under the sink, a toilet that overflows, or a heater that is cold. Say whether water is still flowing and whether you have shut off the main valve.

If you smell gas, do not stay on the phone inside. Leave the building, call 911 from outside, and then call the gas utility before any plumber.

What happens after you hang up

.

You will be told a clear price before work starts, and a licensed and insured plumber does the job. We offer same-day appointments when available, but we do not promise a particular arrival time.

Keep the water off and your phone nearby, since the plumber may call to confirm the address or find the shutoff.
