Water Softener Salt in Los Gatos: Which Type and How Much

.

Most Los Gatos softeners run well on high purity pellets or crystals, and the brine tank should stay at least a third full. How fast you burn through salt depends on your water hardness, which differs across the San Jose Water sources serving the area.

.

Softener salt looks like a simple purchase, yet the wrong bag can cause a clogged brine tank and hard water at the tap. Salt recharges the resin beads that pull calcium and magnesium out of your water. If the salt cannot dissolve or sits on a crust, the resin never gets a full recharge.

The right choice depends on your softener model, how much salt it holds and how hard your water is. The sections below cover the common types, a simple refill routine and the points where a plumber should take over.

Pellets, crystals or solar salt?

.

All three are sodium chloride. They differ in purity, shape and how they behave in a damp tank.

TypeBest forWatch out for.
Evaporated pelletsMost household softenersHigher cost per bag, but cleaner tank.
Solar salt crystalsSofteners that tolerate more residueMore insoluble dirt left at the bottom.
Rock saltOnly if your manual allows itHeavy sediment that can clog the tank.

Follow the softener maker's manual first. It overrides any general rule.

Pellets are compressed from evaporated salt and dissolve cleanly, which is why many softener makers recommend them. Solar crystals are cheaper but carry more insoluble material that settles as a gray silt on the tank floor. Rock salt carries the most debris and is the likeliest to cause trouble.

Los Gatos local note

What Los Gatos hardness means for salt use

San Jose Water covers Los Gatos and lists three source types in its 2025 report. Mountain surface water averages 151 ppm hardness, or 9 grains per gallon. Groundwater averages 288 ppm, or 16.8 grains per gallon, with results from 175 to 451 ppm. Valley Water treated surface water averages 99 ppm, or 5.8 grains per gallon.

Harder water means the resin saturates faster, so the softener regenerates more often and uses more salt. Which source reaches your tap depends on your area, so test your tap with a hardness kit or ask San Jose Water before you set the hardness dial. A softener set for 5.8 grains and fed 16.8 will leave hard water in the house.

How much salt should the tank hold?

.

Keep the salt level above the water line, and refill when the tank drops to about a third full. Do not fill to the brim. Brine needs room, and an overfilled tank makes it harder to spot a bridge or a mush layer.

Check the level once a month, since a softener on harder water will empty a bag faster than one on softer water. If the level has not dropped in two months, something is wrong, because the unit is not drawing brine.

Why does salt bridge or turn to mush?

.

A salt bridge is a hard crust that forms above the water, leaving an empty gap beneath. The tank looks full, but no salt reaches the water, so the resin never recharges. A mush layer is a sludge of recrystallized salt on the bottom that can plug the pickup line.

How to check for a bridge

.

Push a broom handle gently into the salt. If it hits a hard layer and you can feel open space below, break the crust with the handle. Do not use a sharp tool that could crack the tank. Humidity, overfilling and mixing salt types all invite bridging.

How to clear mush

.

Scoop out the loose salt, empty the brine tank if you can, rinse it and refill with fresh pellets. If the unit sits in a damp garage or crawlspace, buy smaller bags and use them quickly.

A salt refill routine you can follow

.

This takes a few minutes and keeps the resin recharging properly.

Monthly softener check

  • Lift the brine tank lid and check the salt level against the water line..
  • Probe for a bridge with a broom handle..
  • Look for gray silt or mush at the bottom..
  • Add salt of the same type you used before, since mixing can cause clumps..
  • Check that the control head shows the right hardness setting..
  • Taste or test the water. Hard, soapy or spotty water means a problem..

When to call a plumber about a softener

.

Call if the unit cycles constantly, leaks around the control head or drain line, or leaves hard water even with a full tank. Those problems point to worn resin, a failed valve or a clogged injector. A plumber for water softener and filtration work can test hardness at the tap and the outlet to see what the resin is doing.

Replacing a water softener with the same type in the same location needs a Town permit that can be applied for online. If you are adding a unit or changing where it sits, the plumbing changes may need a regular building permit, so ask the Town building division. For new supply and drain connections near the garage or laundry, a plumber who handles plumbing installation can plan the layout.
