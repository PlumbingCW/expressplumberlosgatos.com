Which sewer district serves your Saratoga address?

.

Saratoga does not have one sewer provider. West Valley Sanitation District collects sewage for about two thirds of the city, and Cupertino Sanitary District serves roughly the other third, including some hillside areas. Published reviews do not map which streets fall where.

So the first question on a sewer call is simple. Ask the districts, or ask us when we arrive, which one owns the main on your street. It matters because the responsibilities differ. Cupertino Sanitary District is responsible for both mains and laterals, while in West Valley territory the lateral is typically the homeowner's.

Hillside septic tanks and sewer connections

.

Some hillside parcels and small pockets inside both districts are not on sewer at all. They use private septic tanks. Slow drains, gurgling and soggy ground near the tank field mean something different than they do on a sewer line, and a hydro jet is the wrong tool for a failing leach field.

If you are on septic, tell the dispatcher when you call. For owners within West Valley, the rule is that septic owners generally must connect when seeking a building permit with sewer available, or when a sewer lies within 300 feet. Ask the sanitary district before you plan a remodel.

Before we arrive in Saratoga

Saratoga lots range from small valley-floor parcels to large hillside properties, so the main shutoff can be far from the house. Locate it now. These four steps apply while our 24/7 line gets a plumber headed your way.

  1. Turn the main water valve clockwise until it stops. On a hillside lot it may be at the meter near the road, so walk down to it if you have to..
  2. Shut off power to wet rooms at the breaker panel, and stay out of standing water near outlets..
  3. Clear the area of rugs and boxes. If a sewer fixture is overflowing, stop running water and flushing..
  4. Call (855) 575-2890, say whether you are on sewer or septic, and describe what you see..

Jobs worth planning for in Saratoga houses

.

Two jobs worth planning for in a Saratoga house are water heater replacement and sewer line repair. Most homes here are single-family detached houses, and a tank in a garage or closet can fail quietly until a puddle shows. When we replace one, we also check the shutoff, the expansion tank and the drain pan.

On the sewer side, a camera tells us whether the problem is roots, a belly in the line or a broken joint before anyone digs. If you only need the line cleared, a sewer cleaning visit may be enough.

Older parts of the Village and nearby streets can call for a hidden leak check when the water bill climbs and nothing is visibly wet.

Foothill fire risk, drought and your plumbing

.

The county wildfire annex describes Saratoga as a wildland urban interface with very high fire hazard severity zones, and notes water shortages at higher elevations during drought. For plumbing, that means two habits. Know where your shutoff is, and fix running toilets and dripping outdoor valves promptly, since every leak counts when supply is tight.

San Jose Water Company is the retail supplier. After a wildfire evacuation or a long absence, run cold taps for a minute and check under sinks for dampness before using hot water.
