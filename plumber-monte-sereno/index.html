Monte Sereno has no shops, offices or factories. The General Plan describes a city that is entirely residential, with one to five homes per acre. For plumbing, that means every call here is a house call, and the pipe runs are often longer than you would find in a tract neighborhood.

The city's single-family zones set minimum lots of 8,000, 21,780 and 43,560 square feet. On a half-acre or full-acre parcel, the water service line can run a long way from the street meter to the house. A slow pinhole leak in buried pipe can run for weeks under a lawn before anything shows. If your water bill climbs and the lawn stays soft, call us for leak detection before you dig anything up.

What hillside and creek lots do to your pipes

.

The Hillside neighborhood has moderate to steep slopes, woods and narrow winding roads. Drain lines on a slope need steady fall, and a settled or root-bent section shows up as a toilet that gurgles or a tub that drains slowly after a dry spell. In the Foothill neighborhood, San Tomas Aquinas Creek runs through, so lower lots can stay damp and hide small leaks behind the usual wet-ground clues.

Mature oaks and redwoods are everywhere here. Their roots go looking for the moisture in cracked clay or cast iron drain pipe and wrap it. If a camera shows roots in your line, the fix may involve a tree, and the city requires a permit to remove an oak or redwood over 20 inches in circumference. Ask us what the pipe needs first, then call the city before the saw comes out.

Before we arrive in Monte Sereno

A burst line or a flooding bathroom does not wait for business hours, and our dispatch line is staffed 24/7. While you wait for a plumber, a few minutes of work can save a floor.

  1. Find the main water valve, usually at the house wall or in the meter box, and turn it clockwise until it stops. On a large lot the meter box may be a long walk from the house, so grab a flashlight at night..
  2. Switch off power to wet areas at the breaker panel, and stay out of standing water near outlets and appliances..
  3. Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet zone, and lay towels along a hallway so water does not follow the slope of the floor..
  4. Call (855) 575-2890 and tell the dispatcher what you shut off, where the water is coming from and whether your home is on sewer or a septic tank..

If you smell gas, leave the house, avoid switches and flames, and call 911 from outside before you call anyone else.
