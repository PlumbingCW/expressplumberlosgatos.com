Sewer backup in Campbell: who handles what

.

West Valley Sanitation District manages Campbell's sewer system, and Campbell sits fully inside it. The district takes backup and overflow calls around the clock. Its weekday line is (408) 378-2407 with option 2, and after hours it is (408) 299-2507.

Why call them first? The district's code splits the lateral at the property-line cleanout. You own the upper lateral, from the building to that cleanout, and the district owns the lower lateral from the cleanout to its main.

With no cleanout, or a lateral that runs through an easement, you are responsible all the way to the main. Backup crews check the district main first. If the blockage is on your side, call us.

For a clog on your side of the cleanout, the plumber we send runs a cable first and a camera when the clog returns. When roots or a broken joint show up, sewer line repair is the next conversation. A hydro jet pass clears grease and scale from the pipe wall when a cable only punches a hole.

Drains and water heaters in houses this age

.

In houses of this age, kitchen lines collect grease and scale, and a bathroom branch drain may share a trap arm with two fixtures. A cable passes most of these. If the line backs up again within weeks, drain cleaning should include a camera look to see why.

Tank water heaters in 1960s and 1970s houses often sit in garages or hall closets. Mention it when the water runs lukewarm, the tank rumbles, or you see rust at the base. Replacing a thermostat, heating element or gas valve is a water heater repair. A tank that is leaking from the shell is past repair.

Campbell water, gas rules and the water heater you choose

.

San Jose Water Company supplies Campbell. By its own figures, supply is roughly 40 percent groundwater, 50 percent imported water bought from Valley Water, and 10 percent local mountain surface water. Hardness differs by source. San Jose Water's report puts groundwater at 288 ppm, mountain water at 151 ppm and Valley Water treated water at 99 ppm, so your tap depends on which source serves your address.

Harder water leaves scale on heating elements and tank bottoms. If the tank pops and crackles, scale is a likely cause. Ask your plumber about flushing, and consider water softener or filtration if scale keeps returning.

One more Campbell detail. A city ordinance bars NOx emissions from water heating appliances in new buildings and substantial rebuilds, so a gas replacement may not be allowed in those projects. Before you buy a tank for a remodel, ask the City of Campbell Building Division. Permits and inspections go through the MGO Connect online platform.

Creekside streets and what to check

.

Valley Water has marked flood-prone areas along San Tomas Aquino Creek upstream of Williams Road, including Campbell Avenue, Hamilton Avenue and Payne Avenue. If you live near those streets, a few checks are worth doing before winter.

Checks for flood-prone Campbell addresses

  • Pour a gallon of water into the floor drain or sump pit and watch that it empties..
  • Ask whether your sewer line has a backwater valve, and where its access cover sits..
  • Test that the sump pump float rises freely and the discharge line is not kinked..
  • Look at the lowest drains in the house, since they back up first when the main surcharges..
  • Keep the main shutoff clear of storage so anyone in the house can reach it..
