Water is coming through the ceiling right now

Stay calm and work from the source outward. These four steps limit damage while you wait for a plumber.

  1. Turn the main water shutoff clockwise until it stops. It is usually at the front of the house, in the garage or by the water heater..
  2. Switch off power to any room where water is dripping from a light fixture or pooling near outlets..
  3. Move rugs, electronics and furniture out of the wet area, and set a bucket under any active drip..
  4. Call (855) 575-2890 and describe where the water is coming from, so the right tools come with the plumber..

Who owns the sewer pipe in Los Gatos?

.

A backed-up toilet or a tub that fills with gray water often means a sewer problem, and the first question is whose pipe it is. West Valley Sanitation District covers Los Gatos, Campbell and Monte Sereno, along with about two thirds of Saratoga. Its code makes the upper lateral, from your house to the property line cleanout, your responsibility.

The lower lateral, from that cleanout to the public main, belongs to the district. If your property has no cleanout, or nobody knows where it is, the district says the owner is responsible all the way to the main. That is worth finding out before you pay for digging.

The district asks residents to call it first about a backup and to stop using water until it clears. Its crews check the main and clear the lateral from the cleanout if one exists. Anything past that point needs a plumber, and that is where sewer line cleaning and sewer line repair by the plumber we send come in.

Hard water and the age of your water heater

.

San Jose Water serves Los Gatos, along with Campbell, Saratoga and Monte Sereno, and its report lists three supply types. Mountain surface water averages 151 ppm hardness, groundwater averages 288 ppm, and treated Valley Water supply averages 99 ppm. Which one reaches your tap varies, so test your own water before you buy anything.

Harder water leaves more scale on heating elements, tank bottoms and tankless heat exchangers. A tank that rumbles when it heats, or a tankless unit that throws errors, may be fighting scale. A heater repair visit can flush the tank and check the anode rod, while a water softener addresses the water itself.

Water heater permits and earthquake straps

.

Replacing a water heater with the same type in the same spot needs a Town of Los Gatos permit, and the Town takes permit applications through its online portal only. If you change the type or move the unit, the quick replacement application does not apply and a regular building permit is needed.

The Town's bracing handout calls for at least two restraints, one in the upper third and one in the lower third of the tank. When we quote a water heater replacement, the straps, drain pan and shutoff valves belong in the price, so you are not surprised after the old tank is out.

When the cause is gas, not water

.

A rotten-egg smell near a water heater or furnace is a safety problem before it is a plumbing one. Leave the building, avoid light switches and anything that could spark, and call 911 from a safe distance. Then call the gas utility, and only after that call a plumber.

If a gas shutoff is closed, the utility must be called to turn the gas back on. A plumber can repair the water heater, the flue connection or the gas line once the area is cleared.

Questions that decide repair or replace

.

A clear price starts with a few facts. How old is the equipment, where exactly is the leak, and is the damage still spreading? A pinhole in an accessible copper line is a short repair, while a tank leaking from the shell is almost always a replacement. Pipe, drain and fixture jobs have the same split.

Cost depends on access, materials, pipe length and whether a permit is needed. Our leak detection and burst pipe repair pages explain how those factors change the work, and the plumber we send goes over them with you before anything starts.
