How our plumbing services are grouped

.

The list below is organized the way homeowners describe their trouble. Leaks and pipes cover anything wet in a wall, floor or yard. Water heaters cover tanks and tankless units. Drains and sewer cover clogs from a single sink to the main line, while fixtures and installs cover the faucet, toilet and new-line work that fills the rest of a normal year.

Services marked 24/7 are urgent by nature, such as a burst pipe or a sewer backup. Planned jobs like repiping, a water softener or a new water heater install are scheduled at a calmer pace, which also gives you time to compare options.

Repair, replace or inspect first?

.

A good plumber finds the cause before recommending the fix. A repeat clog may need a camera look, which is why sewer inspection sits beside drain cleaning in the list. A slow leak in an unknown spot starts with finding the leak, not a hole in the wall.

Age and material matter too. Galvanized steel and old copper with repeated pinholes may be cheaper to replace than patch, and that is the point where repiping enters the conversation. A tank heater that is leaking from the shell is a water heater replacement job, since the steel itself has failed.

Local rules that shape the work

.

Two Los Gatos details change how some jobs go. West Valley Sanitation District owns the lower sewer lateral, while the upper lateral from the house to the property line cleanout is yours, so a sewer repair starts with knowing where the cleanout is. The Town of Los Gatos also requires a permit to replace a water heater, and applications go through its online portal.

Water quality plays a part as well. San Jose Water reports different hardness figures by supply source, so scale on a tankless heat exchanger or in a tank can differ from one street to the next. The plumber we send tests and asks rather than assumes.

Every service on this page is dispatched to ZIP codes 95030, 95031, 95032 and 95033 around Los Gatos, and the service areas page lists the neighboring towns.
